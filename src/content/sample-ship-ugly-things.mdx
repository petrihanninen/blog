---
title: Ship Ugly Things
date: 2026-09-30
lede: The polish you are so proud of is usually the last thing anyone notices.
---

Every project I have abandoned had one thing in common. It looked great in the first week and never shipped. Every project that actually did something useful for someone started out looking like a mess.

### Why the polish comes too early

Polishing is comfortable. It has a tight feedback loop and nobody can tell you that your drop shadow is wrong. Solving the actual problem does not have that luxury. You can spend a day on it and end up with nothing to show.

So we polish first, tell ourselves we are "setting up the foundation", and run out of steam before the hard part even begins.

### What to do instead

- Build the ugliest possible version that does the whole job end to end
- Show it to one real person
- Only polish the parts that person actually touched

That is it. I have written about the [two hour rule](#) before and this is the same idea from a different angle.

> If you are not embarrassed by the first version of your product, you've launched too late.

The quote is overused for a reason.

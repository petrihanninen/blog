---
title: You Need Taste
date: 2026-10-14
lede: Why would anybody ask something from you instead of asking it from their favorite AI agent? Because you have taste that the AI doesn't.
---

Probably most of us have wondered, if AI will take our jobs. If you do nothing but grab the Jira ticket that somebody wrote, copy-paste it to your agent and forward the results, it definitely will. Or if it won't, the company you work for will be outpaced by a competitor, and the result will be the same.

How do you avoid this fate then? By bringing something that an AI can't: your taste. Any unique experiences and expertise you have gathered can be combined to create your own vision you and your team should be taking. Then you're not just a meat proxy, but somebody who helps shape the future and thus provide an alternative PoV that any general LLM can't.

To gather the experience, you need to gain smoe battle scars. Build stuff, break stuff, figure out what went well and what did not. Learn new things, run experiments and gain perspective. Do not just restrict yourself to code stuff either. Solve issues in your day to day. Read a book on business, design or whatever domain you care about. Write about you experiences, record a video or have a chat with a friend over coffee or beer. Every single thing you do is a step forward.

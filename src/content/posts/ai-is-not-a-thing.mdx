---
title: AI Isn't a Single Thing to Argue About
lede: Most of the public discussion around AI combines and mixes up different, barely related topics. This makes any intellectual discussion and argumentation all but impossible.
date: 2026-10-24
---

If we want to actually tackle the issues, raise awareness, reap the benefits or just have any sensible discussion related to AI and its effect on us and the society, we need to define what we are talking about. Here is a short list of distinct topics, that all need exploring, analysing and considering but that shouldn't be mixed.

## AI's Impact on the Day-to-day of a Knowledge Worker

How do you use AI at work? Will you become more productive? If some things in your work become faster and are not the bottleneck anymore, where do the bottlenecks shift?

## AI as a Feature in an Already Existing Product

Do you already provide a product or service, and have you / the company you work for been doing that for the past 5 or 50 years? What are the new features and/or improvements to your current features that help you provide even more value or capture even broader market? Where can you embed AI to make your customers' experience even better?

## AI as the Core Product of your Service

Did you start a new business on an opportunity that is only possible because what you can achieve with LLMs and other AI tools? What are the things that the world has never had that nwo could become a reality?

## AI Providers

Is your company actually providing LLMs or related tools to others? Do you work at Anthropic, OpenAI or such?

## AI Takes Our Jobs

If some jobs become more efficient to do, some people will lose their jobs. What can they do instead, will Jevon's paradox come to the rescue? Will there be a new group of people struggling to apply their old-world skills to this post-AI world?

## Copyright Issues

Have the LLMs used been trained in lawful and morally acceptable ways? Should the original creators be compensated?

## Information Security and AI Hacks

How should we deal with the fact that AI is used to break the security in pretty much every software on the planet and probably in space as well. Should the frontier models be guardrailed to avoid this, and if, how?

date: 2025-06-27

Books I actually finished this summer, in the order I read them. No affiliate links, no ratings.

- *The Mythical Man-Month*, again. Still right about almost everything, still ignored by almost everyone.
- *The Rock Warrior's Way*. A climbing book that is mostly about fear. Applies to pull requests more than it should.
- *Thinking in Systems*. Short, dense, worth reading twice.
- *A Philosophy of Software Design*. The chapter on deep modules changed how I split code.
- *Piranesi*. Fiction. Read it in one sitting on a train.

Half-finished and probably staying that way: a 900-page history of the Roman Republic, and a book on category theory that I keep opening and closing.

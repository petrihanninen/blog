---
title: Notes on Finger Strength
date: 2026-08-12
---

Two months of hangboarding, three sessions a week. Max hang on the 20 mm edge went from body weight plus 10 kg to body weight plus 24 kg. More importantly, nothing hurts.

What worked: short sessions, long rests, never going to failure. What did not work: adding hangs on climbing days because I "felt fresh".

Writing it down so I do not forget it the next time I feel fresh.

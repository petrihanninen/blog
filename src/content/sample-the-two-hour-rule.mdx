---
title: The Two Hour Rule
date: 2026-05-19
lede: If a side project cannot show something after two hours, the scope is wrong.
---

Two hours is enough to learn whether an idea has legs. It is not enough to build anything serious, and that is the point. The constraint forces you to pick the smallest thing that could possibly be interesting.

### How I apply it

Start a timer. Decide what "something" means before touching the keyboard. When the timer rings, stop and look at what you have. Then decide whether the next two hours are worth it.

Most of the time they are not, and that is fine. The ideas that survive a few rounds of this tend to be the ones worth finishing.

### The failure mode

The rule falls apart when "something" quietly grows while you work. A login screen is not something. A working list that does not look good is.
